Kunci Karakter Konsisten: Master Character Sheet AI

Pelajari formula paten membuat lembar acuan karakter multi-sudut pandang agar tokoh animasi tidak berubah wajah, rambut, dan pakaian di setiap adegan.

Tujuan modul ini: Menguasai struktur prompt 3 panel (tampak depan, close-up dada, tampak belakang), memilih rasio 16:9 yang tepat, dan menghasilkan acuan visual siap animasi.

Contoh Character Sheet 3 Panel dari Studio Asdos: Tampak Depan Full Body, Close-Up Dada, dan Tampak Belakang.

1
Kenapa Karakter Sering Berubah?

Jika langsung meminta AI menggerakkan adegan tanpa acuan baku, generator AI akan mengacak ulang proporsi dan busana tokoh di tiap render.

2
Solusi: 1 Lembar Acuan (Turnaround Sheet)

Dengan 3 sudut pandang dalam 1 canvas horizontal, AI memiliki panduan utuh 360 derajat untuk adegan gerak apa pun.

Langkah 1 · Alur Praktik Cepat

5 Langkah Praktik Menghasilkan Character Sheet

Ikuti urutan langkah standar ini baik di ChatGPT, Google Flow (Nano Banana / Imagen), maupun Gemini.

1
Salin Rumus Prompt Paten

Gunakan format baku studio Asdos yang memisahkan deskripsi karakter dan layout studio lighting.

2
Buka Generator AI Pilihan

Buka ChatGPT (DALL-E 3), Google Flow (Menu Text to Image), atau Google Gemini.

3
Isi Deskripsi & Pilihan Gaya

Tuliskan ciri khas tokoh (usia, pakaian, warna rambut) serta gaya visual (3D Pixar, Realistik, Anime, atau Clay).

4
Pilih Ukuran Rasio 16:9 (Horizontal)

Wajib pilih rasio melebar (landscape 16:9) agar ketiga panel tampak depan, close-up, dan belakang muat rapi berdampingan.

5
Klik Generate & Simpan Gambar

Simpan hasil terbaik ke galeri HP untuk digunakan sebagai acuan visual di tahap animasi gerak.

Langkah 2 · Formula Universal

Formula Prompt Paten Studio Asdos

Struktur baku berstandar industri dengan layout 3 panel terpisah garis tipis, pencahayaan studio netral, dan negative prompt pembersih distorsi.

Master Template Prompt Universal
# CHARACTER DESCRIPTION [Masukkan deskripsi karakter secara lengkap di sini] # STYLE/GAYA [Photorealistic / Pixar-quality 3D / Stylized 3D Cartoon / Anime / Clay / Low Poly / Disney-inspired / dll] --- Create a professional character sheet of the character described above in the specified style. ## Character Consistency - Preserve every detail from the Character Description. - Maintain identical facial features, hairstyle, body proportions, clothing, accessories, colors, and materials across every view. - The character must be exactly the same in all panels. ## Layout - Split the canvas into 3 equal vertical panels separated by thin black divider lines. - Left Panel: Wide Shot — Full Body Front View. - Center Panel: Close-Up Portrait — Chest Up, facing directly forward. - Right Panel: Wide Shot — Full Body Back View. - Ensure the entire body is visible from head to toe in both wide shots. ## Background - Pure solid neutral grey background. - Flat single-color background. - No gradient. - No texture. - No floor. - No wall. - No environment. - No reflections. - No objects. - No props. ## Lighting - Flat professional studio lighting. - Uniform illumination from every direction. - No dramatic lighting. - No directional lighting. - No hard light. - No rim light. - No volumetric light. - No ambient occlusion. - No facial shadow. - No nose shadow. - No eye shadow. - No chin shadow. - No clothing shadow. - No cast shadow. - No background shadow. ## Camera - Orthographic presentation for the full-body views. - Front view perfectly centered. - Back view perfectly centered. - Close-up portrait centered with an 85mm portrait lens look and minimal distortion. - Eye-level camera. ## Pose - Neutral standing pose. - Relaxed natural posture. - Looking straight ahead in the front views. - Back view facing directly away. - Neutral facial expression. ## Image Quality - Extremely clean presentation. - High-resolution. - Professional character turnaround sheet. - Sharp details. - Symmetrical composition. - Consistent proportions. - Production-ready character reference. ## Negative Prompt shadow, cast shadow, facial shadow, background shadow, floor shadow, gradient background, textured background, environment, props, furniture, text, logo, watermark, caption, cropped body, duplicate character, extra limbs, deformed anatomy, inconsistent clothing, blur, noise, low quality

Langkah 4 · Solusi Masalah

Aturan Rasio 16:9 & Solusi Kendala

Pahami perbedaan antara rasio generate acuan awal (16:9) dengan rasio video animasi akhir (9:16 vertikal).

Kunci Penting: Generate character sheet wajib dalam format 16:9 (Horizontal) agar ketiga panel tampak utuh. Saat membuat klip animasi di Google Flow barulah crop foto tokoh menjadi 9:16 (Vertikal).
Tambahkan ciri fisik yang spesifik di `# CHARACTER DESCRIPTION`. Misalnya: "mata hitam bulat, poni rambut belah samping, tahi lalat di pipi kanan, memakai kacamata frame hitam". Deskripsi spesifik memaksa AI menjaga konsistensi antarsudut.
Pastikan baris `## Background` dan `## Negative Prompt` tidak terhapus. AI butuh instruksi tegas: "Pure solid neutral grey background, no floor, no shadows, no texture" agar latar benar-benar bersih dan mudah dipotong.
Posisikan tangan dalam keadaan santai di samping pinggang ("hands relaxed at sides"). Hindari pose mengepal atau gestur rumit saat generate character sheet pertama kali.
Gunakan kalimat "Ensure the entire body is visible from head to toe with comfortable margin" dan pastikan rasio gambar diatur ke 16:9 atau 3:2 landscape.
